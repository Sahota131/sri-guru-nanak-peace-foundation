One Humanity. Boundless Compassion.

Sri Guru Nanak Peace Foundation is a federally incorporated Canadian not-for-profit organization. Its incorporated purposes focus on poverty relief for vulnerable children, education for children and youth in financial need, and vocational skills training for low-income individuals.

Our guiding philosophy is service beyond boundaries: dignity, compassion and opportunity for all, regardless of faith, nationality, gender or background.

Humanitarian Priorities

Our Broader Values

Respect for Humanity and Nature

Guided by Gurbani, the Foundation values the equality of all people, the dignity of women, and responsible care for air, water and earth.

Environmental stewardship and women's equality are guiding commitments. Any independent future programs in these areas would require review against the Foundation's governing purposes.

Mission Saanjh ਸਾਂਝ

A shared commitment to humanity, compassion, connection and service.

Saanjh is a Punjabi word expressing togetherness, sharing and a sense of belonging. As an initiative of Sri Guru Nanak Peace Foundation, Mission Saanjh reflects the spirit of working together for the dignity and well-being of all people, without distinction.

Rooted in the universal message of Sri Guru Nanak Ji, Mission Saanjh invites people from different backgrounds to build understanding, extend a helping hand and strengthen community bonds.

Details of Mission Saanjh activities and opportunities will be announced as they are confirmed.
