Support Our Humanitarian Mission

Contributions can help advance the Foundation's intended work in poverty relief for vulnerable children, educational opportunities for children and youth in need, and vocational skills development for low-income individuals.

Child Welfare

Basic necessities such as food, clothing, shelter and healthcare for orphaned and vulnerable children, particularly in India.

Education and Livelihoods

Educational assistance, tutoring, supplies, scholarships and skills training aligned with the Foundation's incorporated purposes.

Donation arrangements are being established. No online payment facility or verified donation account is currently provided on this website. Please do not send money to an account or payment link claiming to represent the Foundation unless its authenticity is independently confirmed.

Tax receipts: Federal not-for-profit incorporation does not, by itself, confer CRA registered-charity status. The Foundation does not currently claim authority on this website to issue official charitable donation receipts.
