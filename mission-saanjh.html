Mission Saanjh ਸਾਂਝ

A shared commitment to humanity, compassion, connection and service.

Saanjh is a Punjabi word expressing togetherness, sharing and a sense of belonging. As an initiative of Sri Guru Nanak Peace Foundation, Mission Saanjh reflects the spirit of working together for the dignity and well-being of all people, without distinction.

Rooted in the universal message of Sri Guru Nanak Ji, Mission Saanjh invites people from different backgrounds to build understanding, extend a helping hand and strengthen community bonds.

Details of Mission Saanjh activities and opportunities will be announced as they are confirmed.
